Magpie

Communicate better. Get where you were going.

Magpie is one place for everyone you talk to, on whatever they use to reach you, and for your whole life rather than only the working part of it. It keeps track of what you said you would do, who is waiting on you, and what you are trying to get to, so conversations carry things forward instead of burying them. This is the Magpie desktop app.

What it is for

Everyone, in one place

Every channel you talk on, read as one set of conversations with the history that belongs to each person, instead of one app per network and you doing the joining up.

Nothing dropped between you

What you promised, what you are owed, and who has gone quiet. Magpie keeps the thread of it so you do not have to hold it in your head.

Pointed at what you are trying to do

What you are working towards sits alongside the conversations that move it, so talking to people is how things advance rather than what interrupts them.

Opening it the first time

On a Mac

Open the .dmg, drag Magpie to Applications, and open it. It is signed with our Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so there is no warning to click through. After that it updates itself.

On Windows

Windows will stop the installer with "Windows protected your PC". That is expected: the build is not code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Signing needs a certificate whose key lives on dedicated hardware, which is a purchase and a company verification we have not done yet. Until then the Windows build also does not update itself — come back here for a new one.
